Our story

A garden you can sit inside, built by hand

Birds & Bees Cafeto sits at Phooti Kothi Circle in Scheme 71, inside the Orange Square Building. Step in from the road and the traffic goes quiet behind a wall of creepers.

The space was built with upcycled materials and handmade decor: wicker lamps, shelves of planters, a ceiling of greens. Food is fresh and 100% vegetarian, from Indori club sandwiches to nine-inch pizzas and thick Nutella cold coffee.

Green everywhereFerns, creepers and potted plants at every table.
Upcycled & handmadeDecor made by hand, much of it from reused materials.
Pure veg kitchenEvery dish and drink on the menu is vegetarian.

Questions, answered

      
Anything else? WhatsApp us on 98260 33910 or call 098263 47009.

    

    

      
Is everything vegetarian?
Yes. Birds & Bees Cafeto is a pure vegetarian cafe. Every dish and drink on the menu is veg.

      
What are your hours?
We're open every day from 10:30 AM to 11:00 PM.

      
Where exactly are you?
Phooti Kothi Circle, Scheme No. 71, Gumasta Nagar, Indore 452009. We're in the Orange Square Building, near Brand Factory.

      
How much does a meal cost?
Most guests spend ₹200–400 per person. Pizzas start at ₹150, sandwiches at ₹100 and mojitos at ₹130.

      
Do I need to book?
Walk-ins are welcome. For evenings, weekends or bigger groups, send a WhatsApp request above or book through Zomato, Swiggy Dineout, EazyDiner or District.

      
How do I order at my table?
Open the order section, tick what you want, choose your table number (1 to 10) and tap Place order. The order reaches our WhatsApp with your name and table, and we bring it over. Pay at the counter.

      
Can I order food home?
Yes, we're listed on Zomato for delivery.
